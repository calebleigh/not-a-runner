Not a Runner: privacy

Your training data is yours. This page explains what the app does with it.

Health Connect and Apple Health

If you connect it, the app reads your daily steps, workouts (type, time, distance), heart rate during workouts and weigh-ins. It uses them only to fill in your training log. It never writes to Health Connect or Apple Health, and never sells, shares or uses this data for ads.

Where your data lives

Everything is saved on your device. If you turn on sync and sign in with Google, your log is also stored in your own private account space (Google Firebase) so your devices match. Nobody else can read it. Signing out stops sync.

Your choices

You can turn off Health Connect in Settings at any time, change what the app can read in Health Connect, export or import all your data, and stop sync whenever you like.
